Biblioteca / Scriptorium.ro - Usage Terms
==========================================

This site publishes public-domain and freely-licensed literary texts for
reading, linking, and citation.

You may:
  - Read, browse, and link to any individual page.
  - Quote, cite, and reference individual texts for scientific, academic,
    or personal research use.
  - Retrieve a reasonable number of pages for such use (as a rule of
    thumb: up to roughly a thousand page requests a day from one source).

You may not, without prior written permission:
  - Systematically download, scrape, or mirror the collection, in whole
    or in large part.
  - Use the content, or bulk-harvested copies of it, for commercial
    purposes, including training or fine-tuning machine learning models.
  - Redistribute a bulk copy of the collection.

Automated access that ignores robots.txt, or that otherwise behaves like
a bulk mirroring tool rather than an individual reader, may be blocked
without notice.

Questions or requests for a bulk-access license: contact the site owner
via scriptorium.ro.
